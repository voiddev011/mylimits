Good to know

A few clear answers.

What to expect from the beta.

Do I need a MyLimits account or server?

No. Browser tracking runs locally with your existing provider sign-in. Optional snapshot sync in a configured build is a separate, explicit choice and requires its own server and MyLimits authorization.

Which tools can I track?

Claude, ChatGPT / Codex and Cursor have beta website readers. The optional local helper supports independent Codex and Claude Code accounts. GitHub Copilot is coming soon. The extension also includes Gemini local activity observations and Grok / Perplexity readers; availability and quota coverage vary by source and plan.

Does it show every ChatGPT chat limit?

No. The ChatGPT website reader focuses on the Work / Codex allowance. Ordinary chat quotas are separate, and missing limits are not estimated.

Will it keep tracking after I sign out?

Website tracking depends on your browser’s provider session. Sign-out, expiry or a provider challenge can interrupt it. Optional recovery can attempt a brief background tab, but cannot sign in for you. Independent accounts through the Desktop helper use separate official CLI profiles; their sessions can also expire or be revoked.

Are saved sign-ins available?

Not in the current production policy. Every provider’s saved-sign-in vault is disabled. The code contains an opt-in encrypted local vault for future gated use; its presence does not mean independent website sign-ins are currently supported.

Does MyLimits read my conversations?

It does not collect prompts or conversations. Selected session and usage interfaces supply identity and normalized usage. Activity-only observation checks an allowlist of request URLs, methods and success statuses, not request or response bodies.

Why is a reading stale or a reset unknown?

The provider may not expose that information, your login may have expired, or a check may have failed. MyLimits retains the last successful reading with its age. A countdown reaching zero alone is not evidence that the provider reset a quota.

Is my data encrypted?

Normal readings, nicknames, history and settings are local, but are not an encrypted vault. The disabled saved-sign-in implementation encrypts credentials separately. Desktop profiles use official CLI credential storage, whose protection depends on the platform. Privacy mode hides screen content; it does not encrypt your data.

How do I delete an account’s data?

Use Forget account to remove its saved readings, nickname and history. Uninstalling removes the extension’s browser storage. Optional synced copies and Desktop profiles require separate removal. See the deletion instructions.

How do I report a problem?

Open a GitHub issue. Include your browser, extension version and a description of the problem. Do not post tokens, cookies, sign-in links or private account exports.
